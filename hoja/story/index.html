1980 — 1989

Otero Ramos Arquitectos

Our journey began in 1980, when architecture-school friends Arq. Andrés Otero and Arq. Jorge Ramos founded Otero Ramos Arquitectos — a partnership that lasted a decade and established the principles that still drive the firm: architectural excellence through technical innovation, delivered with personal service.

  • .01Puerto Rico Telephone Company exchange buildings across the island
  • .02150+ gas stations built and remodeled for Esso and Shell; 20+ for Texaco
  • .03Hospital Hermanos Meléndez, Bayamón
  • .04Hospital Guadalupe expansion, Hato Rey
  • .05Schools in Bo. Guanábano, Aguada
  • .0620+ private residences in San Juan, Guaynabo and Dorado

1990 — 2009

Andres Otero & Associates → Servicios Técnicos y Desarrollos

In 1990 Andrés Otero founded Andres Otero & Associates, which in 1995 became Servicios Técnicos y Desarrollos Inc. — the venture under which the Architechnika brand was born. Andrés Otero Jr. began his architecture studies in this era and started steering the firm toward new technology, laying the bedrock for its next evolution.

  • .01Bayamón Medical Plaza and Hospital Hnos. Meléndez expansions
  • .02Retail programs: Sam's, Builders Square (Costco), Kmart, Marshalls
  • .03Hatillo, Vega Baja & Quebradillas municipal coliseums
  • .04Master plans, terminals and malecón for municipalities island-wide
  • .052,700+ dwellings of social-interest housing
  • .06Travenol clean-room facilities, Barceloneta

2010 — Today

ArchiTechnika, Inc.

In 2009, ArchiTechnika Inc. was born: father and son as partners, and a new way of engaging the design process. An early adopter of 3D, BIM and project-management platforms, the firm became a lean boutique studio that handles projects of any scale through technology and strategic partnerships — every project receiving the same dedication and attention to detail.

  • .01Hotel conversions and repositionings across Condado & Old San Juan — Kimpton, Aloft, Selina, Ascend
  • .02HiBird Apartment & Suites Hotel, Ashford Avenue
  • .03Normandie Hotel repositioning proposal
  • .04Luxury residences at Dorado Beach and Palmas del Mar
  • .05Work across Puerto Rico, Nebraska and Florida
  • .06Master plans: Ciudad Roberto Clemente, Guaynabo Urban Center

Continuidad / Continuity

The letterhead changed four times. The line man never did.

Luis I. Tua joined Otero Ramos Arquitectos in 1980 as its first line man and has developed the construction documents for more than three hundred projects since — through every name on the masthead, every change of software, and one complete migration from vellum to BIM. Andrés Otero Sr. has practised across the whole span. Andrés Otero Jr. brought the model.

That is the entire explanation for how a studio this size delivers a hospital campus in Bayamón and a ten-thousand-seat coliseum in Quebradillas: nobody has had to be told twice how the firm draws.
