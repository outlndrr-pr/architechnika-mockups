A-900.03 · NOTES TO BIDDER

01

Bring the difficult one

The building that already exists, the site that fights back, the schedule that was set before the design was. Those are the projects this office is organised around.

02

Scale is not the question

Nine rooms on a mountaintop or ten thousand seats in Quebradillas — both go through the same model and the same three sets of initials.

03

Say what is undecided

A program with honest gaps is easier to price than one with confident guesses in it. Mark the unknowns; we will schedule them.
